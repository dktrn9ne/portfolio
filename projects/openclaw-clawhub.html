I have worked in the OpenClaw ecosystem from its early Clawdbot period through its evolution into OpenClaw, building reusable skills that package complex technical workflows into agent-ready capabilities. The work combines product thinking, domain expertise, tool integration, and practical agent design.

Published skills

  • FiveM Developer - 3.2K+ downloads. A reusable skill for specialized FiveM development workflows.
  • Mux Video Bot - 2.5K+ downloads. A skill that translates video-infrastructure knowledge into reusable workflows for agents working with Mux.

What I design for

  • Clear tool selection and structured instructions for multi-step agent execution.
  • API-oriented workflows that convert domain knowledge into repeatable actions.
  • Context management, failure handling, and outputs that remain useful outside a single prompt.
  • Iteration based on real-world adoption rather than treating agent skills as one-off demos.
